"They Live Off Our Taxes?" Work, Livelihood and Torah Study

The loudest headline in the media, and what's behind it.

Moshe Ginzy

"They live off our hard-earned tax dollars!" That is the loudest headline in the media. But has anyone ever bothered to look at the real numbers?

Who works, and who studies

The overwhelming majority of religious and traditional Jews work hard, pay taxes, and drive the economy forward every single day. And those who dedicate their entire lives to studying Torah day and night? They are not living in luxury. They live with bare minimums, driven by an unshakeable belief that they are safeguarding our nation's most precious treasure.

Culture costs money

Any modern country spends hundreds of millions on theater, art, and sports because they value culture. So why is it that when it comes to the original culture and heritage of our people, it is suddenly called a burden?

Torah is not a burden on the economy. It is the very reason we exist here.

Let's learn to respect instead of divide.
